Helix Robotics Travel and Expense Policy

Booking: Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance through the company travel portal.

Hotels: Hotel stays are capped at 180 USD per night, or 250 USD in New York and San Francisco.

Meals: The meal allowance is 60 USD per day while traveling.

Claims: Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the trip, with receipts for any item over 25 USD.

Devices: If a company laptop is lost or stolen while traveling, tell the security team as soon as you can and file a police report where it happened.
