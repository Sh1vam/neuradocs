Helix Robotics Employee Handbook

Remote work: New hires may work remotely up to two days per week during their first six months. After six months, managers may approve up to three remote days per week.

Equipment: Every employee receives a laptop and one external monitor. Additional peripherals require written approval from IT.

Leave: Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid leave per month. Unused leave up to 10 days carries over to the next calendar year.

Security: All laptops must use full-disk encryption. Lost devices must be reported to the security team within 24 hours.
