Helix Robotics Internship Program

Duration: Internships run for twelve weeks, starting in June.

Remote work: Interns work on site five days per week for the first four weeks, then may request one remote day per week.

Leave: Interns accrue 0.5 days of paid leave per month. Unused leave does not carry over.

Stipend: Interns receive a monthly stipend, paid on the last working day of the month.
