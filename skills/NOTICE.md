Every other skill here was written for NeuraDocs. `make-presentation`, `make-report`, `make-spreadsheet` and `read-documents`
were written after reading Anthropic's Apache-2.0 document skills (docx, pptx, pdf, xlsx); they are not copies, because those
skills drive tools (docx-js, LibreOffice, helper scripts) that this app does not have.
